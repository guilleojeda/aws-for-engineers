Creating Your AWS Account

To schedule an AWS certification exam, you need to create an AWS account. This account will serve as your gateway to the AWS certification portal, where you can register for exams, access study resources, and track your progress.

Register on the AWS Website

To create an AWS account, follow these steps:

1. Go to the AWS website (https://portal.aws.amazon.com/billing/signup) and click on "Create an AWS account." 2. Enter your account information, including your email address, password, and phone number. 3. Choose a secure and unique password for your root user account. 4. Verify your email address by entering the verification code sent to your email. 5. Provide additional information, such as your name, address, and company details (if applicable).

Important: Use a secure corporate distribution list (e.g., it.admins@example.com) as your email address if this account is for a business. This ensures that your company can retain access to the AWS account even when an employee changes positions or leaves the company.

Using the AWS Portal

To schedule your exam, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your AWS certification account using your credentials. 2. Click on the "Schedule New Exam" button and select the exam you want to take. 3. Choose your preferred testing option: online proctoring or testing center. 4. Select your preferred date and time for the exam. 5. Review and agree to the exam policies and procedures. 6. Pay for the exam registration fee using a valid payment method.

Additional Considerations

Cookie and Privacy Settings

When scheduling your AWS certification exam, it's essential to manage your cookie preferences on the AWS certification website. Time Extensions for Non-Native Speakers

If you're a non-native English speaker, you may be eligible for an additional 30 minutes to complete your exam. To request a time extension, contact AWS Training and Certification Customer Service and provide your exam registration details.

Discount Vouchers

AWS offers a 50% discount voucher for your next exam after passing an AWS certification exam. To redeem your voucher, simply log in to your AWS Certification Account, select the exam you want to register for, and apply the voucher at checkout.

How difficult is the AWS exam?

The AWS certification exams are scenario-based and will thoroughly test your knowledge and expertise in this field. They require more than just reading books, watching videos, and taking a few practice exams. Professional help is recommended to pass AWS certification tests.

How do I schedule my exam?

You can schedule your exam at aws.training. Select to schedule with Pearson VUE and then choose the “At my home or office” option. Due to high demand, you may experience longer wait times for an appointment than usual.

Yes, you can get a discount on your exam fee if you have already had the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner certificate or have passed any AWS exam.
